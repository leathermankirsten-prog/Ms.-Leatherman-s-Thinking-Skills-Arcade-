Your mission

Imagine you are aboard a modern cargo ship sailing from the Los Angeles area toward Honolulu. Write a first-person diary entry about one eventful day at sea. Make the reader feel as if they are standing beside you on deck. Use vivid language and include accurate details about cargo ships from the article.

Use details from the article

The article describes the Mahimahi, a container ship traveling between the Los Angeles area and Honolulu. It explains how the ship moves, steers, and responds to wind, waves, and currents. Use the article to make your diary entry believable, while inventing your own narrator and events.

Writing tools: Show, don't tell

Instead of “I was scared,” show a hand tightening around the railing, knees shaking, or a voice becoming quiet. Use precise verbs (lurched, rattled, steadied) and specific adjectives (salt-streaked, towering, slick).

Writing tools: Figurative language and sensory details

Try a simile (“The containers stood like a wall”), personification (“The wind clawed at my jacket”), or onomatopoeia (“CLANG!”). Describe what you saw, heard, smelled, felt, or tasted—only when those senses fit the scene.
