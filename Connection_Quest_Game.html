Teacher Studio

Create a topic with exactly four groups of four. Each group needs a category name, four distinct terms, and a reason they belong together. Custom topics are saved only in this browser unless exported or shared by link.

My custom topics

Teacher note: A shared topic link embeds the selected topic in the URL, so students can play it on another Chromebook without importing a file. Long links may be rejected by some school systems. For a permanent multi-topic collection, export your pack and publish an updated site file.

How to play

  1. Choose a topic.
  2. Tap four ideas you believe have something meaningful in common.
  3. Select Check my four to receive instant feedback.
  4. When you solve a group, explain your reasoning in a complete sentence.
  5. Keep going until all four groups are solved.

Scoring: +25 per correct group, −5 for a hint, −3 for an incorrect guess. You can still finish even after mistakes.

Teacher tip: Project the game for collaborative discussion, or let students work independently. Explanations stay on the student's device and are not collected by a server.
